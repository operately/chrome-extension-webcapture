# Privacy Policy for Capture to Operately

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Capture to Operately is a Chrome extension that creates an Operately task from a webpage selected by the user. This policy explains the limited data the extension handles to provide that feature.

## Data the extension handles

When you open the extension, it reads the title and URL of the active webpage so it can preview the task that will be created. It does not read or collect the page body, form contents, browsing history, passwords, authentication cookies, or content from other tabs.

The extension also requests the Operately account information needed to complete the action, including your identity, companies, active projects, spaces, and task boards. It uses your existing signed-in Operately session but does not read or store your credentials.

The following preferences may be stored locally by Chrome:

- The configured Operately server address.
- The last selected destination.
- A pending page title and URL while you complete sign-in.

## How data is used

The page title and URL are used only to prepare the task shown in the extension. They are sent to your configured Operately server only after you choose **Create task**. The URL is added to the task description and the title becomes the initial task name, which you can edit before submission.

The extension does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, profiling, or data brokerage services.

## Sharing and transfers

Data is sent only to the Operately server you configure. By default, this is Operately Cloud at `https://app.operately.com`. If you configure a self-hosted Operately server, that server is operated by you or your organization and its own privacy and retention practices apply.

The task is visible to people who already have access to the project or space task board you select, according to that Operately workspace's permissions. Creating the task is your instruction to share its title and source URL with those authorized workspace members.

The extension does not sell user data or share it with advertising platforms, data brokers, or unrelated third parties.

## Security

Operately Cloud and production self-hosted servers must use HTTPS. Plain HTTP is supported only for local development servers on `localhost` or `127.0.0.1`.

## Retention and control

Locally stored preferences remain in Chrome until they are replaced, cleared, or the extension is removed. Tasks created through the extension are retained by the configured Operately server according to that server's policies and can be edited or deleted in Operately.

## Chrome Web Store Limited Use disclosure

The use of information received from Chrome APIs adheres to the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/user-data-faq), including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide or improve the extension's single purpose and is not used for personalized advertising, creditworthiness, lending, or sale to third parties. Human access is prohibited except with the user's explicit consent for support, when required for security, to comply with applicable law, or for aggregated and anonymized internal operations permitted by the policy.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and material changes will be disclosed before new data practices take effect.
